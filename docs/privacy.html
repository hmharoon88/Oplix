Privacy Policy

How we handle your information

Last updated: May 2026

Introduction

Oplix ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and services.

By using Oplix, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information we collect

Personal information

We collect information that you provide directly to us, including:

  • Account information: Email address, username, password, and role (manager or employee)
  • Profile information: Name, location assignments, and employee details
  • Business information: Location data, employee records, task assignments, and shift information
  • Financial data: Payroll information, sales data, expenses, and register data
  • Content: Task completion photos, lottery forms, and other user-generated content

Automatically collected information

When you use Oplix, we may automatically collect:

  • Device information (device type, operating system, unique device identifiers)
  • Usage data (features used, time in app, interaction patterns)
  • Log data (IP address, access times, error logs)

How we use your information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve our services
  • To authenticate users and manage accounts
  • To process business operations (shifts, tasks, payroll, etc.)
  • To communicate with you about your account and our services
  • To ensure security and prevent fraud
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To analyze usage and improve user experience

Data storage and security

Your data is stored using Firebase (Google). We implement technical and organizational measures including encryption in transit and at rest, secure authentication, role-based access, and regular security updates.

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We strive to protect your information but cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information when:

  • Within your organization: Managers and authorized personnel access relevant business data
  • Service providers: Third parties such as Firebase/Google assist in operating our services
  • Legal requirements: Required by law or valid legal requests
  • Business transfers: In a merger, acquisition, or asset sale

Your rights and choices

  • Access: Request access to your personal information
  • Correction: Update information through the app
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and data
  • Opt-out: Stop using the app and request data removal

Contact us using the information below to exercise these rights.

Third-party services

Oplix uses Firebase for authentication, storage, and cloud services. Your use is also subject to Google's privacy practices.

Firebase Privacy Policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Data retention

We retain personal information as long as needed to provide services and fulfill this policy. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize data except where retention is required by law.

Children's privacy

Oplix is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children. Contact us if you believe we have collected a child's information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact us

Email: support@oplix.app

Response time: Typically within 24–48 hours
