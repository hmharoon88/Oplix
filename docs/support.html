Frequently asked questions

How do I log in as an owner?

Use the Owner option on the login screen with your registered email and password.

How do employees clock in and out?

Open the Clock In/Out section while logged in. You must be assigned to a location and follow your site's clock-in rules.

How do I add a new location?

As an owner, go to Locations and tap the + button. Enter the location details and save.

How do I fill out a lottery form?

Employees must be clocked in first. Open Lottery, choose Active Shift, and complete the required fields.

I forgot my password

On the login screen, tap Forgot Password? and follow the email instructions to reset it.

How do I manage employees?

As an owner, open Employees, select a location, then add, edit, or manage staff for that site.
